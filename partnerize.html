The Repeating Flywheel

Every campaign makes the
next one smarter.

The EchoSignal → EchoBoost loop isn't a one-time activation. Every campaign generates new performance data that sharpens the signal for every brand and publisher in the Partnerize network.

Step 01
EchoSignal watches
Monitors click velocity, CVR, and content performance across every Partnerize publisher simultaneously. Flags the amplification window the moment it opens — 48h refresh cycle, network-wide.
Step 02
EchoBoost deploys
Brand budget activates against the flagged content. Best retailer destination locked in at launch. Campaign runs on Meta, TikTok, Pinterest — wherever the audience lives. Spend only against proven signal, never cold.
Step 03
Publisher earns more
Creator or publisher earns incremental commission on every sale driven by the boosted campaign — on top of their existing organic earnings. New revenue stream, same content, no extra work.
Step 04
VantagePoint measures
Full-funnel attribution captures the journey — including zero-click AI-assisted conversions. Data feeds back into EchoSignal. The next window is detected faster, scored more accurately, and boosted with sharper targeting.
For Partnerize brands: an on-demand media buying center that activates against their existing affiliate network — no new vendor relationships, no new tracking setup.  ·  For Partnerize publishers: incremental earnings on content they've already created.  ·  For Partnerize as a platform: a managed media revenue layer sitting above the SaaS and commission infrastructure already in place.
