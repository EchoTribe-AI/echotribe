01 — Window Types
Four windows.
One signal.

EchoSignal detects four distinct amplification window types — each requiring a different deployment strategy. Knowing which one you're in changes how you act.

01 — Deal Windows
Limited-time offers, narrow window
A time-limited offer drives elevated click velocity. The window is narrow — often 12–24 hours before the deal expires. Spend deploys immediately or not at all.
02 — Seasonal Windows
Calendar-driven intent peaks
Summer planning, holiday gifting, back to school. Predictable timing lets you stage campaigns before the spike rather than react to it — when CPMs are lower and intent is rising.
03 — Restock & Trending
Scarcity and momentum windows
A product approaching restock scarcity, or a trending moment creating unusual velocity. Narrow window, high CVR. EchoSignal flags the moment — EchoBoost closes it.
04 — Pattern Windows
Recurring signals become playbooks
A recurring weekly or monthly signal that becomes a repeatable campaign. A post that spikes every Thursday becomes a standing playbook — not a one-time event. The most compounding window type.
