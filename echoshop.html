EchoShop doesn't generate generic storefronts. It builds pages around your feed's best-performing products — with copy, imagery, and routing already dialed in before the creator hits approve.

01 — Auto-Assembly
From feed to live page in minutes
EchoShop ingests your affiliate product feed, selects the best-performing SKUs for the content context, and assembles a complete landing page — images, descriptions, pricing, retailer badges, and CTA — without a single line of code.
02 — Dynamic Content
Copy and imagery that converts
Every assembled page gets AI-written product descriptions tuned for conversion — not just lifted from the manufacturer feed. Images are sourced from the best available creative, prioritizing lifestyle over spec shots.
03 — Smart Routing
Every product link routes to the highest EPC
EchoShop doesn't just build pages — it wires every buy button through EchoRoute, so each click routes to the retailer paying the highest commission right now. No manual link management. No stale affiliate codes.
Creator Approval Flow
Creators see exactly what goes live — before it does.

Every auto-assembled page enters a creator approval queue. The creator sees the full page: product cards, AI-generated caption in their voice, retailer routing, and pricing. One click to approve, one click to edit. Nothing publishes until they say so.
